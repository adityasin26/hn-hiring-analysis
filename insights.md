# Key Insights: Hacker News "Who is Hiring?" (Sep – Nov 2026)

All figures below were independently verified across Python (`pandas`), MySQL, and Power BI.

## 1. Monthly Hiring Volume & Remote Stability
Hiring volume remained consistent across the 3-month window, with remote roles making up nearly half of all postings each month:

| Month | Total Posts | Remote Posts | Remote Share |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| September 2026 | 261 | 124 | 47.5% |
| October 2026 | 273 | 128 | 46.9% |
| November 2026 | 243 | 116 | 47.7% |
| **Total** | **777** | **368** | **47.4%** |

## 2. Remote Policy Breakdown
* **Remote:** 368 posts (47.4%)
* **Onsite:** 156 posts (20.1%)
* **Hybrid:** 138 posts (17.8%)
* **Not Specified:** 115 posts (14.8%)

## 3. Role Demand & Remote Flexibility
* **Software Engineering** is the largest hiring category, appearing in **417 posts (53.7%)**.
* Software Engineering roles are significantly more likely to be remote (**55.2%**, or 230 of 417 posts) compared to the overall dataset average (**47.4%**).

## 4. Skill Demand
* **AI** is the #1 most frequently mentioned skill across the entire dataset, appearing in **401 posts (51.6%)**.
* **Customer Success / Support:** AI is also the top-mentioned skill in Customer Success / Support roles (**22 posts**), highlighting that SaaS and tech companies increasingly expect CX and Support teams to work with AI products and workflows.
